# XXD Panel 063 Original prompt · English reading translation

Please make each photo I upload into an independent high-end design poster. No splicing of multiple pictures, and each photo will be output separately. The overall composition adopts a 3:4 vertical version. The upper part retains the original photo, maintaining the main structure, real texture, natural light and shadow, and original color atmosphere. Only slight advanced photography color correction is performed to give it the texture of art magazine and exhibition photography. In order to adapt to the frame, the environmental background can be naturally expanded, but the subject must not be stretched, distorted, or changed.

The lower part extracts the most identifiable ** subject, outline, posture and narrative relationship ** in the photo, retains only the most metaphorical, emotional or aesthetic core themes of the original picture, and reconstructs it into a ** pixel mask and color block collage picture ** with a sense of editorial design. Don’t completely copy the scene, but look for a large outline, negative shape or symbolic form that is the most memorable as a visual container, and then compress the key objects, local structures, actions and environmental clues in the original image into a small number of pixelated graphics, forming smart nesting, blocking, echoing and metaphorical relationships.

All graphics adopt a clear logic of pixel blocks and summarize the shapes with stepped edges, square modules, simplified color planes and a small amount of misaligned splicing; even if they are highly simplified, the most critical proportions, postures and characteristics of the original objects must still be retained so that each pixel shape can be recognized. A very slight glitch is allowed locally: pixel shifts, breaks, small pieces of misalignment or partial loss, but it is only for rhythm and a sense of surprise, and does not destroy subject recognition, nor does it turn into cyber glitch art.

The color matching extracts the most comfortable and vital color relationships from the original image and transforms it into a **soft, transparent, slightly desaturated limited color palette **. Several coordinated large color blocks are used to establish layers, and a very small number of characteristic colors in the original image are used as visual jump points. The background color is not fixed. According to the comprehensive color temperature of the original image, it is intelligently matched to an **extremely light, almost white comprehensive color background **. It can be slightly blue, green, pink, beige, gray or other original image hues, and retain very slight paper particles, making the picture clean, soft and breathable.

The composition emphasizes **a core mask, a small number of pixel shapes, a large area of ​​white space and a clear visual hierarchy**. The subject does not have to be centered, but can be offset, cropped, suspended or partially out of bounds; the color blocks establish rhythm through scale, spacing, positive and negative shapes and pixel edges to avoid even coverage and regular nine-square grid, making the blank space itself an important part of the picture.

There is no fixed template for text and no language restrictions. Based on the real theme, action, memory, emotion or metaphor of the photo, freely extract a small number of meaningful words or phrases and type them together with the pixel structure; the text can be hidden in negative shapes, arranged along the edge of the mask, aligned with pixel blocks, misplaced or partially cut off by graphics, making the copy a part of the meaning of the picture, rather than an additional explanation.

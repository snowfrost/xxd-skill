## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design compresses the source into one embroidered mission patch / technical identification badge: twill fabric ground, raised satin-stitch lettering, fill embroidery, thick merrowed edge, institutional naming, medium-small scale and warm-white breathing space. It is not a photographic embroidery filter, a filled realistic scene, a tiny rubber stamp, a smooth vector, a plastic merch mockup, a candy-coloured souvenir or a templated cultural-product shot. Extract the brightest living colours from the source and recompose them as clear sky-blue, lake cyan, fresh green, warm yellow, coral and peach-pink against pure warm white.

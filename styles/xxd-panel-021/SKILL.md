# XXD Panel 021 · 黑色矩形越界手绘编辑

Turn each supplied photograph into a finished monochrome editorial illustration. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame places the same recognisable subject or inseparable relation mostly inside one pure-black horizontal or vertical rectangle while one source-defining feature emerges, passes through, grows beyond, or breaks its edge. The stable heavy field and free rough contour must form one inseparable composition.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; source-adaptive ordinary canvases; exact 50/50 paired geometry; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections may refine aesthetics, copy hierarchy, and acceptance criteria but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve identity, silhouette, pose, action, function, and relation through at least three source-specific cues; keep most of the subject inside one pure-black rectangle and make one most distinctive feature cross its boundary. Use black hand line, white negative shape, and only tiny grey areas. It is not a subject pasted over a rectangle, pure silhouette, smooth 022 linework, polished vector, realistic rendering, 3D, or generic icon.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 021 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relation → preserve at least three identity cues and one principal action → choose one horizontal or vertical pure-black rectangle as the stable visual field → keep most of the subject inside it → choose one most characteristic head, top contour, branch, roof, limb, object edge, or other structure as the boundary-breaking focus → make that feature genuinely emerge from, pass through, or grow out of the black space → draw only essential contour and structure in slightly varied black ink, technical pen, or photocopy line with jitter, local breaks, repeated tracing, and human error → use white negative form plus very small grey planes → offset, crop, and surround the field with generous white paper → integrate one short title plus one to three tiny editorial notes along the field edge, negative shape, breakout direction, or contour alignment**.

The humour and strangeness come from the source's actual contour crossing a rigid geometric law—not from stock cute faces, mascots, or props. Ink drawing, technical-pen sketches, photocopy line, experimental editorial illustration, and high-end art publishing are construction influences rather than templates.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the rectangle orientation, subject placement, breakout feature, contour, white negative shape, or copy. Also reject colour, broad grey modelling, smooth stable 022 line, uniform vector outlines, pure black silhouette, dense scenery, several focal points, glossy polish, photoreal shading, 3D volume, and template centring.

## 021 visual system

### One subject crossing one black field

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across contour, pose/orientation, function, proportion, material, colour, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, or relationship distance.
- Choose one horizontal or vertical pure-black rectangle from the subject's weight, direction, and contour. Keep most of the principal subject or inseparable relation inside it, and select exactly one most characteristic local structure to cross the boundary.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth gesture; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and negative shape; landscapes keep one source-specific terrain or spatial relation.
- The subject must feel as if it emerges from, passes through, or grows out of black space. Never paste a complete outlined figure on top of a decorative rectangle. Secondary marks only clarify gesture, distance, field edge, platform, or direction.

### Rough line, white negative shape, tiny grey

- Use pure white paper, one flat pure-black rectangle, black hand-drawn contour, white negative form, and only small grey planes. No colour, broad wash, gradient, or realistic modelling.
- Line resembles ink brush, technical pen, or photocopy drawing: slightly varied in weight, lightly jittery, locally broken, sometimes retraced, and visibly human. It must remain legible without becoming smooth, stable, or polished.
- Preserve only essential outer contour, pose, and key structure. Use a few expression lines, construction lines, folds, or local shadow marks; never fill the whole subject as a solid silhouette.

### Geometric order versus free contour

- The rectangle is a stable heavy base; the subject uses asymmetric placement, local crop, and breakout to create life. Surround it with generous white paper.
- Keep black, white, and grey area relationships strong and restrained. A few lines, planes, platforms, edges, or geometric slices may support the main relation but never create another subject.
- Reject complex scenes, equal-weight props, decorative borders, realistic light, polished outlines, and template-like centred page furniture.

### Absolute monochrome hierarchy

- White stays clean and largest; the black field stays perfectly decisive; grey remains minor. There is no coloured accent.
- Recognition comes from contour, white negative shape, breakout geometry, line rhythm, and a few black details—not tonal rendering.
- The result feels humorous, restrained, intelligent, slightly strange, and art-published without becoming cute merchandise, horror, or a generic logo.

## Copy and typography belong to 021

021 text is a light black-and-white editorial annotation system belonging to the rectangle-and-breakout composition—not a headline wall, UI label, signature, or detached overlay.

- Automatic copy derives one short title from visible action, emotion, state, relation, or grounded metaphor, then adds one to three micro-annotations, indexes, state words, or phrases.
- A grounded quote-like line, number, or chapter mark may appear, but factual-looking details must be supplied or reliably established. Never invent dates, locations, provenance, or catalogue facts.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 021 construction:

- **Role:** mark the breakout direction and complete the tension between geometric order and free contour.
- **Voice:** restrained, intelligent, witty, slightly strange, and non-promotional.
- **Construction:** one short title plus one to three tiny groups in black handwriting, slender sans, or restrained serif.
- **Placement:** attach to the black-field edge, hide in negative form, follow the breakout direction, or align and misalign with the subject contour.
- **Material:** black ink or clean editorial print with slight human irregularity; always small in scale.
- **Script:** use a native hand-made editorial equivalent for the resolved locale. Preserve natural Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Latin construction rather than imitating Latin handwriting across every script.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.

## Raster generation and privacy

Use the host's built-in bitmap image-generation capability by default and follow the available `imagegen` skill when exposed. View each local source before generation. Use one generation call per distinct asset; a wallpaper pack requires four calls. “Black field”, “boundary-breaking contour”, “ink line”, “technical-pen line”, and “photocopy editorial” describe appearance only and never authorise SVG, HTML, CSS, Canvas, diagrams, or programmatic drawing as the artwork.

If built-in bitmap generation is unavailable, use `scripts/configured_imagegen.py`, which reuses an already configured compatible bitmap route and emits sanitised status only:

```bash

  --prompt-file /private/job-temp/transform-prompt.txt \
  --out /private/job-temp/design.png --size 1536x1024 --quality high
```

Judge readiness by actual bitmap capability, not a provider name or one environment variable. Never display, log, persist, or report providers, endpoints, headers, credentials, account identifiers, route configuration, prompts, or response bodies. Do not manually inspect credential files. The bridge's sanitised JSON is the complete allowed diagnostic surface.

An explicit invocation of `/xxd-panel-021` or `$xxd-panel-021` with a source image and requested output authorises generation through any already configured authenticated bitmap route available to the session. Do not ask for a second confirmation solely because the route changes. This does not authorise changing credentials, providers, billing, accounts, or global settings.

Only report generation as unavailable after both built-in capability and the bundled probe fail to establish a usable bitmap route. State the limitation narrowly and never guess the cause or identify the provider. Never substitute code-rendered art.

## Fresh-task and source boundary

Every invocation starts a fresh generation job unless the user explicitly asks to continue, audit, compare, edit, or reuse a named earlier result. Repeating the same source and parameters still means generating a new result in a new task directory. An old file can never satisfy a new request.

Use only images attached to the current invocation, explicit user paths, or a previously supplied image that the current request clearly identifies as “the same image”. Never broadly scan the Desktop, workspace, `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-021/`, or historical task folders to find a substitute source. If the intended source cannot be accessed, ask for it.

## Workflow

   ```text

   1. 常用设备预设
      手机 1440×3200｜iPad 2048×2732｜电脑 3840×2160｜儿童手表 1024×1024
   2. 自定义分辨率
      请分别输入手机、iPad、电脑、儿童手表的尺寸
   ```

5. Open one fresh job boundary and reserve the next unused sibling output directory for every source and selected mode. Confirm only current user-supplied sources, inspect each separately, and read the full prompt matching the working language:
   - Chinese: [references/xxd-panel-021-prompt.zh-CN.md](references/xxd-panel-021-prompt.zh-CN.md)
   - English: [references/xxd-panel-021-prompt.en.md](references/xxd-panel-021-prompt.en.md)
6. Privately lock: the principal subject or inseparable relation; at least three identity, silhouette, pose, action, function, or relation cues; rectangle orientation and area; subject-inside-field ratio; exactly one defining breakout feature; emergence/passage/growth relation; rough ink or photocopy line character; white negative-shape plan; tiny grey roles; asymmetric crop; supporting line/plane restraint; and title plus one-to-three microtype path. Do not invent unsupported biography, location, events, ownership, emotion, symbolism, dates, provenance, or catalogue facts.
7. Lock one copy package per source and share it verbatim across selected modes by default; honour explicit per-mode overrides. Priority: explicit text-free request > exact user wording > editable user direction > source-derived automatic copy. Automatic copy uses one title plus one to three micro-annotations. Do not reuse one photo's copy for another unless asked.
8. Run `scripts/compose_panel.py --plan` for every selected mode and resolved canvas. Selected wallpaper-pack uses four `design-only` plans.
9. Generate each distinct transformed design alone at the planned aspect and preferably exact frame size. For paired modes, prepare the photographic panel separately. Never ask the model to generate both halves at once.
   Within the same current invocation, ordinary modes may reuse one newly approved transformed intermediate only when frame dimensions, aspect ratio, source facts, aesthetic instructions, typography, and locked copy are identical. Otherwise generate separately. Wallpaper assets are always generated separately.
10. For `independent`, generate four wallpapers directly from the source. For `linked`, generate and visually approve the anchor first; every derivative receives exactly the original source plus that same anchor. The source locks identity and content; the anchor locks only black-field orientation, subject-inside ratio, breakout grammar, rough ink/photocopy line, white-negative and tiny-grey hierarchy, asymmetric white-space logic, and typography. Never chain derivatives.
11. Finalise every selected mode in menu order with `scripts/compose_panel.py`, reopen each PNG at normal and thumbnail size, and apply the acceptance gate. Retry only the faulty generated asset once when a hard invariant fails. After one failed correction, return the best result and name the unresolved issue rather than pretending success.

## Composition commands

```bash
# Source-adaptive top-bottom
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --plan --layout top-bottom --source photo.png
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout top-bottom

# Source-adaptive left-right
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --plan --layout left-right --source photo.png
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout left-right

# Design-only
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout design-only

# One wallpaper, repeated separately for all four devices
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --design phone.png --out wallpaper-phone.png \
  --layout design-only --size 1440x3200
```

`--size WIDTHxHEIGHT` overrides `--canvas` and source adaptation. Generate the design at the exact planned aspect; if exact pixels are unsupported, use a closest supported same-aspect bitmap and resample proportionally without cropping. Audit with the same layout used to build the result.

# XXD Panel 003 · 公共议题观念线条

Turn each supplied photograph into finished conceptual editorial artwork. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame must compress the same source's identity, direction, connection, contradiction, pressure, and possible release into a sparse system of continuous black hand-drawn lines. The result stays recognisable without tracing, remains quiet but tense, and lets meaning emerge through entanglement, restriction, passage, tightening, and release.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; source-adaptive ordinary canvases; exact 50/50 paired geometry; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections may refine aesthetics, copy hierarchy, and acceptance criteria but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve source identity through at least three specific cues while converting direction, connection, contradiction, pressure, and turning point into sparse conceptual black line. It is not tracing, decorative one-line art, a generic icon, cartoon, glossy vector, photoreal painting, 3D render, or unrelated flourish.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 003 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relationship → lock its defining direction, connection, conflict, pressure, and turning point → compress them into one coherent system of continuous black hand-drawn lines → enter from an edge, coil/cross/fold/tighten low or off-centre, then lift upward or outward → let a source-bound symbolic contour appear within the motion without fully enclosing or explaining it → preserve cold-white, cream, or pale low-saturation space → add only functional knots, barbs, thorns, or short cross-strokes at real force points → make any type a tiny trace inside the line system**.

The meaning comes from the source's actual forces and relations, not from adding stock protest icons, tears, cages, fists, hearts, mascots, or unrelated symbols. Modernist conceptual posters, public-issue imagery, art publishing, and experimental editorial design are construction influences rather than templates.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the line route, crossings, pressure points, symbolic contour, or copy. Also reject decorative looping, calligraphic prettiness, smooth vector polish, heavy comic outlines, literal background reconstruction, multicolour filling, photoreal shading, 3D volume, dense scenery, commercial advertising, or symbols not earned by the source.

## 003 visual system

### Source forces and emergent contour

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across contour, pose/orientation, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, relationship distance, or dominant direction.
- Identify the actual direction, connection, contradiction, pressure, and turning point in the source. Convert those facts into the route and behaviour of the line rather than illustrating them with stock symbols.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth gesture; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and negative shape; landscapes keep one source-specific terrain or spatial relation.
- Keep one principal subject or inseparable relationship. Its symbolic contour should emerge from the moving line and remain perceptible at a glance without becoming a complete closed outline or literal diagram.

### Tension, route, and release

- Use cold white, cream, or a pale low-saturation ground with overwhelming open space. Place the active system low, lateral, or near an edge—never centred, enlarged to fill the canvas, or balanced like a logo.
- Let one or a small family of continuous black lines enter from a canvas edge, coil, cross, fold back, and tighten before finding an upward or outward release.
- Every crossing, reversal, compression, and release must correspond to a source fact. The route should be quiet yet tense, simple but not easy.
- Preserve breathing room as active silence. Do not add colour fields, decorative curves, generic rays, grids, dots, or symbols simply to enrich the image.

### Pressured black hand line

- Keep the line black or near-black, predominantly fine, with slight changes of width, friction, hesitation, breaks, and pressure. It may feel like taut wire, a path, a restraint, or a wound without literally becoming those objects.
- Add thorns, knots, barbs, or short cross-strokes only at a genuine point of force, crossing, or directional change. They are structural punctuation, never ornament.
- Allow faint ink absorption and paper tooth, but no broad watercolour painting, coloured-pencil fill, pastel blocks, multicolour accents, or simulated glossy wire.
- Preserve flat depth. Reject realistic modelling, gradients, cinematic light, smooth Bézier perfection, metal 3D, clay, resin, plastic, and shadow effects.

### Near-monochrome ground

- Use cold white, cream, or one pale low-saturation ground selected to respect the photograph's atmosphere, with black or near-black line as the visual system.
- If a user explicitly requires colour, allow at most one extremely quiet source-derived tint in the ground or one tiny evidence-led mark; it must not compete with the black line.
- Avoid multicolour fill, gradients, rainbow or brand palettes, decorative accent colour, muddy faux-vintage filters, and atmospheric shading.

## Copy and typography belong to 003

003 text behaves like one tiny trace inside the line system—not a headline, slogan wall, UI label, data display, or detached editorial block.

- Automatic copy derives one very short theme word or phrase from the source's grounded issue, relation, pressure, passage, or release without inventing an advocacy position.
- Prefer one short theme word or phrase plus at most one tiny supporting element such as a supplied number, verified place, or brief note. A year appears only when supplied or reliably established.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 003 construction:

- **Role:** a near-invisible editorial trace that calibrates issue and scale without explaining the image.
- **Voice:** restrained, sober, humane, quiet, and slightly hand-made.
- **Construction:** one fine, compact theme word or phrase and at most one much smaller support element. No bold headline, expressive display type, stretched letterforms, or decorative hierarchy.
- **Placement:** place it in open space near one bend, crossing, or release point, while keeping it subordinate to the line and away from identity cues.
- **Material:** fine absorbed ink or a lightly imperfect printed line consistent with the black drawing.
- **Script:** use a native hand-made editorial equivalent for the resolved locale. Preserve natural Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Latin construction rather than imitating Latin handwriting across every script.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.

## Raster generation and privacy

Use the host's built-in bitmap image-generation capability by default and follow the available `imagegen` skill when exposed. View each local source before generation. Use one generation call per distinct asset; a wallpaper pack requires four calls. “Hand-drawn”, “flat”, “Bauhaus-like”, and “editorial illustration” describe appearance only and never authorise SVG, HTML, CSS, Canvas, diagrams, or programmatic drawing as the artwork.

If built-in bitmap generation is unavailable, use `scripts/configured_imagegen.py`, which reuses an already configured compatible bitmap route and emits sanitised status only:

```bash

  --prompt-file /private/job-temp/transform-prompt.txt \
  --out /private/job-temp/design.png --size 1536x1024 --quality high
```

Judge readiness by actual bitmap capability, not a provider name or one environment variable. Never display, log, persist, or report providers, endpoints, headers, credentials, account identifiers, route configuration, prompts, or response bodies. Do not manually inspect credential files. The bridge's sanitised JSON is the complete allowed diagnostic surface.

An explicit invocation of `/xxd-panel-003` or `$xxd-panel-003` with a source image and requested output authorises generation through any already configured authenticated bitmap route available to the session. Do not ask for a second confirmation solely because the route changes. This does not authorise changing credentials, providers, billing, accounts, or global settings.

Only report generation as unavailable after both built-in capability and the bundled probe fail to establish a usable bitmap route. State the limitation narrowly and never guess the cause or identify the provider. Never substitute code-rendered art.

## Fresh-task and source boundary

Every invocation starts a fresh generation job unless the user explicitly asks to continue, audit, compare, edit, or reuse a named earlier result. Repeating the same source and parameters still means generating a new result in a new task directory. An old file can never satisfy a new request.

Use only images attached to the current invocation, explicit user paths, or a previously supplied image that the current request clearly identifies as “the same image”. Never broadly scan the Desktop, workspace, `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-003/`, or historical task folders to find a substitute source. If the intended source cannot be accessed, ask for it.

## Composition commands

```bash
# Source-adaptive top-bottom
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --plan --layout top-bottom --source photo.png
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout top-bottom

# Source-adaptive left-right
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --plan --layout left-right --source photo.png
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout left-right

# Design-only
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout design-only

# One wallpaper, repeated separately for all four devices
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --design phone.png --out wallpaper-phone.png \
  --layout design-only --size 1440x3200
```

`--size WIDTHxHEIGHT` overrides `--canvas` and source adaptation. Generate the design at the exact planned aspect; if exact pixels are unsupported, use a closest supported same-aspect bitmap and resample proportionally without cropping. Audit with the same layout used to build the result.

## Acceptance gate

Before accepting each result verify:

- Mode, exact pixels, source-adaptive formula, split axis, seam, and output count are correct.
- Visible photography is faithful and type-free; source-hidden outputs contain no source photograph or seam.
- The simplified subject remains recognisable through at least three source-specific cues and preserves the principal action or inseparable relationship.
- Direction, connection, contradiction, pressure, and release are traceable to source facts; no stock public-issue icon or literal explanatory diagram has been added.
- Continuous black line enters from an edge, coils/crosses/tightens low or off-centre, then releases upward or outward; the source-bound contour emerges without a complete literal outline.
- Line friction, hesitation, breaks, and pressure changes are credible. Knots, thorns, barbs, or short cross-strokes appear only at justified force points—not as decoration.
- Ground is cold white, cream, or pale low-saturation with dominant open space; there is no multicolour fill, realistic scene, decorative curve, glossy vector, or 3D wire.
- The composition remains balanced, light, and breathable. Supporting lines and geometric marks extend source movement or meaning rather than filling space.
- The limited source-derived palette stays high-lightness and low-to-medium saturation, with warm paper and a small complementary accent; it is neither muddy nor sugary.
- Automatic copy is concise, grounded, and 003-specific; exact user wording remains verbatim. All rendered text is accurate and native to the resolved locale; text-free output contains no text or pseudo-text.
- Typography remains a fine, near-invisible trace beside one bend, crossing, force point, or release. It never becomes a slogan, explanation, generic bold poster title, UI label, or detachable digital overlay.
- Every wallpaper is separately recomposed, respects safe regions, contains no system UI, and is not a crop of another device result. A linked pack shares one family without drifting from the source.
- Every delivered PNG was newly generated for this invocation and lives in its fresh task directory.

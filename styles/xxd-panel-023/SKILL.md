# XXD Panel 023 · 东方窗形柔光框景编辑

Turn each supplied photograph into one finished soft luminous atmosphere framed by one source-responsive traditional Chinese window or lattice form. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame must keep the subject or inseparable relation clearly tied to the source while it appears within, passes through, occludes, or gently spills beyond the window. Pale source-derived ground, living coloured light, diffuse projection, and quiet space must feel like one modern Eastern memory rather than a decorative architectural template.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; source-adaptive ordinary canvases; exact 50/50 paired geometry; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections may refine aesthetics, copy hierarchy, and acceptance criteria but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve at least three source-specific cues across contour, pose, action, opening, direction, relation, silhouette, material, or light. Choose exactly one window or lattice form from the source structure, then let the subject, branch shadow, light patch, or abstract projection appear within, pass through, occlude, or gently spill beyond it. It is not a fixed moon-gate template, ornamental Chinese border, hard architecture diagram, cartoon, cheap filtered photograph, photoreal painting, or unrelated atmosphere.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 023 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relation → preserve at least three distinctive cues across contour, pose, action, opening, direction, material, light, and relationship → choose exactly one round, patterned, perforated, moon, fan, hexagonal, or simplified lattice window according to those facts → let subject, branch shadow, light patch, or projection appear within, pass through, occlude, or gently spill beyond it → create one principal light zone, one quiet negative-space zone, and a few soft projections as a stable visual triangle → extract one extremely pale low-saturation ground and one living light colour from the source → soften spray grain, pastel dust, air, and diffuse edges while keeping lattice only slightly clearer → integrate one extremely short title plus sparse microtype along the window edge, arc, axis, or light void**.

The atmosphere comes from the source's actual contour, opening, action direction, light, temperature, colour, overlap, and distance—not from pasting every subject into the same moon gate or blue background. Traditional Chinese framed views, sunlight through lattice, pastel airbrush, memory blur, art books, and exhibition catalogues are construction influences rather than templates.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the window choice, subject-window relation, principal light zone, quiet void, projections, ground hue, living light colour, or copy path. Also reject fixed dark blue, dirty grey, heavy darkness, fluorescent colour, hard contrast, hard architectural outline, cartoon contour, cheap filter, ornate lattice overload, perfect symmetry, even filling, realistic impasto, 3D volume, and template repetition.

## 023 visual system

### One source-responsive window

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across contour, pose, action, opening, connection, direction, overlap, material, light, temperature, colour, distance, or asymmetry.
- Choose exactly one round, patterned, perforated, moon, fan, hexagonal, or simplified geometric lattice window according to the locked cues. Never mechanically default to a centred moon gate or stack several window traditions.
- People retain pose, direction, body/clothing mass, and relational distance; animals retain body rhythm and head direction; plants retain growth curve; architecture retains skyline, axes, and defining openings; objects and vehicles retain functional mass and negative shape; landscapes retain a source-specific horizon, terrain curve, or spatial relation.
- Preserve correspondence at a glance without completing every contour. The subject, branch shadow, light patch, or abstract projection must appear within, pass through, occlude, or gently spill beyond the window so frame, depth, and atmosphere become inseparable.

### One light zone, one quiet void

- Compose one principal light area, one quiet negative-space area, and only a few soft projections as a stable poetic triangle.
- The window may be offset, locally cropped, or suspended. Preserve centre of gravity, primary/secondary hierarchy, positive/negative form, overlap, and breathing rhythm without perfect symmetry or even filling.
- Recompose the window, subject relation, principal light, quiet void, and type path for every aspect ratio rather than cropping another output.

### Soft lattice and diffuse projection

- Use fine airbrushed grain, pastel dust, atmospheric soft focus, powder, gentle bloom, and diffused light. Projection edges dissolve naturally instead of becoming hard masks.
- The window or lattice may be slightly clearer than the coloured shadow, but stays soft and atmospheric. Reject rigid architectural drafting, glossy vector outlines, carved 3D ornament, photoreal wall texture, and complex decorative lattice.
- The image should feel like sunlight passing through a traditional window onto a pale wall, then softened by air and memory.

### Source-derived pale ground and living light

- Extract one extremely pale, low-saturation, clean composite ground from the source: mist blue, pale cyan, soft apricot, powder pink, grey green, warm ivory, or very pale violet-grey when supported.
- Distil the source's most vital colour into soft projected light, modestly lifting and purifying it for a restrained warm-cool response. Light colour carries life; the pale ground carries air.
- Keep a gentle but legible value separation between ground, subject, window, and projection. Reject fixed dark blue, dirty grey, heavy darkness, fluorescent colour, harsh contrast, arbitrary rainbow gradients, and global filter tints.

## Copy and typography belong to 023

023 text is a quiet micro-editorial system that aligns with the window, arc, axis, and light void—not a detached headline, generic caption, UI overlay, or pseudo-classical decoration.

- Automatic copy derives one extremely short title from visible or supported mood, time, action, temperature, or metaphor, plus only a few place words, state words, or micro-phrases when useful.
- Dates, places, provenance, and factual numbers must be supplied or reliably established. Never invent them for sophistication. Exact user wording remains verbatim.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 023 construction:

- **Role:** the title carries atmosphere; microtype quietly completes the window, light, and breathing-space order.
- **Voice:** gentle, quiet, lucid, poetic, healing, observant, and source-specific.
- **Construction:** one extremely short title plus sparse microtype in very small sizes and light weights.
- **Placement:** align, orbit, offset, or interweave along a window edge, arc, axis, or quiet light void only when script-appropriate.
- **Material:** slender editorial sans or restrained serif equivalents softened into the atmosphere; no glow, extrusion, sticker effect, pseudo-calligraphy, or generic bold display face.
- **Script:** use native editorial serif and slender-text equivalents for the resolved locale. Preserve natural Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Latin spacing, shaping, orientation, and semantic line breaks rather than forcing Latin tracking or rotation onto every script.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.

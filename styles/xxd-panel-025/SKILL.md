# XXD Panel 025 · 格式塔正负形莫兰迪丝网版画

Turn each supplied photograph into one finished Gestalt screenprint with a double reading. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame keeps one clear source-bound subject silhouette and integrates one closely related hidden environment, structure, object, or symbolic image so the subject reads first and the hidden layer appears only on a second look. Contour interlock, negative-space cutout, shared boundary, and figure-ground reversal must create one inseparable graphic rather than two overlaid objects.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; source-adaptive ordinary canvases; exact 50/50 paired geometry; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections may refine aesthetics, copy hierarchy, and acceptance criteria but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve identity, silhouette, pose, action, and narrative relation through at least three source-specific cues while choosing one source-grounded hidden environment, structure, object, or symbol. Both must interlock as one clear figure-ground graphic with the subject legible first and the hidden image second. It is not two objects side by side, transparent stacking, double exposure, a multi-focus collage, detailed illustration, glossy vector art, photoreal painting, or 3D.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 025 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relation → preserve one clear silhouette, principal action, and at least three defining cues → select one environment, structure, object, or symbolic image most closely grounded in the same source → make their contours bite into each other through negative-space cutout, shared boundary, and figure-ground reversal → ensure the subject reads first from a distance and the hidden image emerges second at close view → keep one visual anchor and generous pale paper → derive two to four clean airy Morandi pastels from the source → keep colour shapes flat and edges crisp while retaining slight paper fibre, screenprint grain, ink coverage, and registration shift → integrate one short title plus sparse microtype into contour, negative boundary, baseline, or whitespace axis**.

The poetry comes from the source's real relation between subject and its environment, structure, object, identity, action, emotion, or grounded symbolism—not from a stock hidden-face trick or arbitrary second icon. Gestalt figure-ground design, screenprint, independent publishing, exhibition graphics, and artist books are construction influences rather than templates.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the principal silhouette, hidden image, shared boundaries, first/second reading order, Morandi palette, or copy. Also reject side-by-side objects, transparent overlays, double exposure, several focal points, dense scenery, direct collage, ordinary gradients, digital blur, cheap ageing, advertising headlines, glossy vectors, realistic shadows, 3D volume, and decoration not earned by the source.

## 025 visual system

### One recognisable silhouette, one hidden image

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across contour, pose/orientation, function, proportion, material, colour, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, or relationship distance.
- Preserve the principal subject or inseparable relation as one decisive clear silhouette with very few edges and no rich illustration detail. It preserves identity, action, function, emotion, and the most important source relationship.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth gesture; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and negative shape; landscapes keep one source-specific terrain or spatial relation.
- Select one source-grounded hidden environment, structure, object, or symbolic image most closely related to the subject. It must deepen the source reading without competing as a second focal point.

### Contour interlock and figure-ground reversal

- Integrate subject and hidden image through contour interlock, negative-space cutout, shared boundary, and figure-ground reversal. One shape's edge must naturally become the other's edge.
- From a distance the principal subject reads first; on closer view the hidden scene or image appears. Preserve this hierarchy at thumbnail and normal size.
- Never place two objects side by side, overlay them transparently, double-expose them, or combine two independent icons. Keep detail low, identity high, and the visual anchor singular.
- Let source pose, direction, weight, and contour flow choose offset placement, local crop, vertical nesting, or edge alignment. Generous pale paper is active pause and discovery.

### Crisp flats and physical screenprint

- Keep contours crisp and colour shapes flat. Retain slight paper fibre, screenprint grain, ink coverage, small registration shift, and subtly uneven inking.
- Physical print evidence stays restrained and never obscures the figure-ground switch or becomes a cheap distressed filter.
- Reject gradients, digital blur, realistic shadows, complex volume, polished CGI, impasto, and indiscriminate damage overlays.

### Two to four source-derived Morandi pastels

- Extract and soften the source's most spirited colour relation into two to four Morandi pastels: mist pink, soft apricot, grey green, sage, pale terracotta, cream yellow, mist blue, grey violet, or other source-supported low-saturation composites.
- Use a pale paper base and establish hierarchy through overall temperature, value difference, and area ratio. Colours stay clean, soft, and airy.
- Avoid dirty grey, heavy ageing, fluorescent colour, high-contrast commercial palettes, rainbow colour, fixed palettes, gradients, and equal colour shares.

## Copy and typography belong to 025

025 text is an intelligent editorial intervention embedded in the positive-negative structure—not a headline pasted into blank space, a commercial slogan, UI label, or detached caption block.

- Automatic copy derives one extremely short title from the photograph's subject, action, relation, environment, and supported second image. It should bind both readings so discovering the hidden form produces an “of course” moment, plus zero to three micro-elements only when they deepen that same meaning.
- Micro-elements may include a tiny note, restrained sentence, chapter mark, catalogue-like code, year, or coordinate-like number, but factual-looking details must be supplied or reliably established. Never invent dates, coordinates, locations, or provenance as facts.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 025 construction:

- **Role:** reinforce the first/second reading switch, shared boundary, interrupted contour, or quiet curatorial axis.
- **Voice:** abstract, literary, curatorial, restrained, precise, and resonant rather than explanatory or promotional.
- **Construction:** one extremely short title plus one to three micro-elements; use a restrained sans, gentle serif, or minimal condensed face with strong scale contrast, light weight, careful spacing, superscript, small caps, or asymmetric alignment when native to the script.
- **Placement:** run along the subject contour, negative-space boundary, horizontal baseline, or whitespace axis; enter the graphic, be interrupted by contour, or occlude with a colour shape only when readability survives.
- **Material:** screenprint or letterpress ink with the same grain, slight misregistration, and imperfect coverage as the image.
- **Script:** use a native high-end editorial equivalent for the resolved locale. Preserve natural Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Latin construction rather than forcing Latin tracking, rotation, small caps, or vertical logic across every script.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.

# XXD Panel 008 · 粉彩不可能空间诗

Turn each supplied photograph into a finished poetic impossible-space scene. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame must translate the same source's most recognisable identity, silhouette, pose, or relationship into an orthographic isometric world of platforms, stairs, doorways, arches, bridges, passages, floating structures, and spatial paradox. Height, path, connection, occlusion, isolation, and repetition carry the story. Luminous dynamic pastels and immaculate matte 3D keep the result bright, quiet, surreal, and source-bound rather than a generic game environment.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; source-adaptive ordinary canvases; exact 50/50 paired geometry; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections may refine aesthetics, copy hierarchy, and acceptance criteria but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve identity, action, function, emotion, and relation through at least three source-specific cues translated into one coherent orthographic isometric spatial system. Geometry, path, height, connection, occlusion, isolation, or repetition must correspond to the source rather than become generic fantasy architecture. It is not tracing, a game screenshot, a stock impossible staircase, textured PBR rendering, photorealism, or unrelated pastel scenery.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 008 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relation → preserve at least three identity, silhouette, pose, action, function, emotion, or relation cues → assign those cues to platforms, stairs, doorways, arches, bridges, passages, floating structures, or impossible connections → use height difference, path, connection, occlusion, isolation, and repetition as narrative grammar → compose in clean orthographic isometric view → choose two to four luminous pastel main colours independently from the source's overall palette, using analogous harmony plus warm/cool contrast → place exactly one higher-purity jump point at a doorway, turn, figure, or decisive structure → render immaculate smooth matte solid-colour 3D with soft even light and only slight ambient shadow → add at most one small geometric editorial line that leaves emotional aftertaste**.

The surreal metaphor comes from converting the source's actual pose, relation, distance, enclosure, direction, repetition, or emotional tension into spatial logic—not from adding arbitrary stairs or borrowing a fixed level layout. Poetic isometric games, minimal surreal architecture, exhibition models, and geometric art publishing are construction influences rather than templates.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the spatial metaphor, route, height relations, anchor geometry, colour jump, or copy. Also reject stock impossible staircases, crowded architecture, dirty or yellowed palettes, low-value fields, large brown or black areas, generic muted palettes, gradients, grain, paper texture, film look, frosted surfaces, old-print effects, complex PBR, harsh reflections, heavy shadows, giant type, and poster slogans.

## 008 visual system

### Source-bound impossible-space translation

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across contour, pose/orientation, function, proportion, material, colour, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, or relationship distance.
- Translate the principal subject or inseparable relation into one coherent spatial proposition. Keep at least three source-specific cues legible through silhouette, path direction, relative scale, openings, connection, separation, repetition, or a small figure.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth gesture; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and negative shape; landscapes keep one source-specific terrain or spatial relation.
- Keep one principal subject or inseparable relationship. Every platform, stair, arch, bridge, passage, or floating structure must clarify it; never add decorative architecture merely to fill space.

### Orthographic isometric narrative grammar

- Use a clean orthographic isometric camera with no photographic perspective distortion. Geometry stays simple, readable, poetic, and quiet.
- Assign narrative roles deliberately: height difference for hierarchy or distance; paths for action or memory; bridges and passages for relation; doors and arches for transition; occlusion for withheld information; isolation for solitude; repetition for rhythm or persistence.
- Permit impossible connections only when they express the source. The paradox must remain visually coherent rather than become a puzzle collage.
- Preserve open air and clear routes. Reject dense cities, labyrinth overload, decorative towers, game HUDs, characters unrelated to the source, and copied level designs.

### Immaculate matte solid-colour 3D

- Keep geometry extremely clean, smooth, matte, and solid-coloured, with precise edges and no visible surface texture.
- Use soft, even illumination and only light ambient shadow to establish depth. Form comes from clean colour planes, height, overlap, and same-hue value steps.
- Keep air clear and detail fine. Reject grain, noise, paper tooth, film look, frosted texture, old-print effects, dirt, scratches, bevel noise, complex PBR, metallic sparkle, wet gloss, and strong reflection.
- Do not simulate a physical miniature photographed in a studio; render a lucid idealised spatial world.

### Dynamic luminous pastel system

- Do not directly sample the photograph's overall palette. Choose two to four main colours per work from luminous cream, pale sandstone, mist pink, coral, soft violet, sky blue, lake cyan, or mint according to the source's emotional structure.
- Build a complete group through analogous harmony and deliberate warm/cool contrast, with large clean colour fields, clear plane separation, and same-hue value steps.
- Add exactly one higher-purity orange-red, magenta, blue-green, or bright-yellow jump at a doorway, turn, figure, or decisive local structure.
- Keep the result bright, soft, transparent-feeling, and airy. Reject muddy grey, yellowed filters, low value, large brown or black fields, and ordinary muted palettes.

## Copy and typography belong to 008

008 text is one small geometric editorial annotation that leaves emotional aftertaste without competing with the spatial world—not a headline, slogan, UI label, or data display.

- Automatic copy derives one short resonant sentence from visible emotion, relation, action, spatial tension, or supported story without inventing biography.
- Use one primary line only. A smaller second-language interpretation appears only when the user explicitly requests bilingual output. Never add a silent English translation.
- Automatic or editable-direction copy never introduces a year, date, number, volume label, catalogue code, or archival marker merely for atmosphere. Exact user wording remains verbatim even when it contains one.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 008 construction:

- **Role:** a small afterimage that completes the spatial metaphor.
- **Voice:** concise, poetic, restrained, intelligent, and lingering rather than promotional.
- **Construction:** thin, simple, geometric editorial type at small scale with generous tracking and native script construction.
- **Placement:** at an edge, corner, architectural void, or lower open area; align, lightly occlude, or subtly interweave with geometry without harming legibility.
- **Material:** perfectly clean flat type from the same immaculate world; never bold display type, handwriting, texture, glow, extrusion, or a giant poster headline.
- **Script:** use a native thin geometric editorial equivalent for the resolved locale. Preserve natural Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Latin construction rather than imposing Latin tracking or letterform logic across every script.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.

  --out result.png --layout top-bottom

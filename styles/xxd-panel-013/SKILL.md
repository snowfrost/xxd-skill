# XXD Panel 013 · 治愈水彩收藏票根

Turn each supplied photograph into one finished collectible watercolour ticket. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame must retain the same source's identity, silhouette, pose, and most memorable subject-to-environment relation inside one horizontal postcard ticket suspended in generous ivory whitespace. The ticket uses a strict large-left/small-right division: about 74% bright, airy source-derived watercolour and 26% quiet locale-native information stub, separated by a precise perforated tear line and held together by one implicit grid.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; source-adaptive ordinary canvases; exact 50/50 paired geometry; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections may refine aesthetics, copy hierarchy, and acceptance criteria but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve source identity through at least three specific cues and keep the source's memorable subject-and-necessary-environment relation inside one centred horizontal ticket. Its internal geometry targets a 74% watercolour scene and 26% information stub, divided by a precise vertical perforation or tear line. It is not an isolated-object vignette, full-bleed generic watercolour, literal photo filter, loose scrapbook, dirty antique ephemera, ecommerce template, photoreal painting, 3D render, or unrelated travel souvenir.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 013 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject and its necessary environment → preserve at least three identity, silhouette, pose, structure, scale, placement, landmark, or narrative-relation cues → choose the one subject–environment relation most worth remembering → simplify it into clear, light, source-bound watercolour rather than isolating one object → place it inside a single centred horizontal matte ticket suspended in ivory whitespace → divide the ticket about 74/26 with a precise vertical perforation → translate the source into two to four bright low-to-medium-saturation neighbouring colours plus at most one tiny warm contrast → keep the subject slightly clearer while distant and secondary environment dissolves → align image, locale-native fields, stamp, number, and rules to one implicit grid → preserve quiet collectible order**.

The ticket comes from the source's actual place, subject, action, season, relation, atmosphere, and memorable environmental cues—not from imposing generic landmarks, airport codes, fake coordinates, stock postal graphics, or a permanent tourism vocabulary. High-end travel postcards, artists' journals, watercolour publishing, vintage ticket logic, and modern editorial grids are construction influences rather than templates.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the watercolour subject, necessary environment, palette, stamp logic, field meanings, or copy. Also reject isolated-object illustration, literal photoreal rendering, full-bleed scenery, muddy greys, yellowed paper, heavy brown, loud colour collision, cheap retro filters, loose floating information, excessive ornament, ecommerce styling, complex realism, and template repetition.

## 013 visual system

### Source identity and memorable relation

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across silhouette, pose/orientation, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, scale, placement, landmark, environmental relation, or dominant direction.
- Identify the relation that carries the memory: person against a street, animal inside weather, object on a table, vehicle within terrain, building in its skyline, plant in light, or another visible source-supported pairing.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and setting; plants keep growth gesture and light; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and placement; landscapes keep source-specific horizon, terrain, path, water, or atmosphere.
- Never isolate the subject when the environment is necessary for recognition or meaning. Simplify secondary detail, but preserve the minimum setting that makes the ticket unmistakably correspond to the source.

### One centred horizontal ticket

- Suspend one horizontal ticket at the visual centre of an ivory or warm-white field. Preserve generous outer whitespace; do not enlarge the ticket to fill the design frame or repeat it as a collage.
- Use thick matte watercolour stock with a fine deckled or micro-serrated edge and only a very light natural contact shadow. The paper feels collectible and tactile, never stained, burnt, cracked, or theatrically aged.
- Keep consistent inner and outer margins. Image, text, stamp, number, short rules, dots, and perforation obey one hidden grid and a small family of baselines.
- The ticket remains the single focus in every aspect ratio. Recompose its scale and whitespace for each device rather than cropping the card or stretching it.

### Strict 74/26 internal geometry

- Allocate about 74% of the ticket width to the watercolour scene and about 26% to the information stub. Keep the left unmistakably larger and the right compact; do not drift toward equal columns.
- Divide them with one precise vertical dashed perforation, tooth line, or tear line. It may meet a stamp or bottom grid slightly, but it remains legible and mechanically aligned.
- The left scene may crop internally like a postcard but must retain the locked subject–environment relationship. The right stub uses disciplined alignment, not decorative scattering.
- Reject equal halves, multiple cards, diagonal ticket splits, irregular scrapbook layers, floating labels, busy borders, seals everywhere, and information that crosses the scene without purpose.

### Bright healing watercolour

- Translate the freshest and most vital source colours into two to four neighbouring high-value, low-to-medium-saturation tones. Sage, mist blue, pale blue-grey, cream yellow, warm sand, and light terracotta are examples, never mandatory defaults.
- Preserve paper white, soft blooms, wet/dry variation, transparent layering, and a little pigment grain. Keep the principal subject slightly clearer; let distance and secondary environment soften naturally.
- Allow only a very small warm contrast when the source supports it. Avoid muddy grey, yellow cast, heavy brown, loud complementary collision, oversaturation, cheap vintage filters, opaque digital fill, and realistic cinematic light.

## Copy and typography belong to 013

013 text behaves like a quiet ticket ledger inside the paper object—not a detached headline, tourism slogan, data dashboard, or decorative pseudo-postmark.

- Automatic copy derives one very short source-specific title or moment phrase and a minimal useful field set. Fields may include locale-native equivalents of DATE, PLACE, SUBJECT, MOMENT, FIELD, and NO., but only when their values are visible, supplied, or reliably established.
- Never invent a date, place, coordinates, route, ticket number, provenance, or travel event. For non-travel sources, replace irrelevant place fields with object, state, project, chapter, season, relation, or emotion records grounded in the image. Abstract index numbers must visibly read as compositional indexes rather than facts.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 013 construction:

- **Role:** a quiet archival information system that turns the watercolour memory into a collectable record without pretending unsupported facts.
- **Voice:** gentle, observant, concise, calm, precise, and lightly poetic.
- **Construction:** one short title or moment phrase, a small field set, one index only when useful, short rules or dot rows, and one restrained circular stamp.
- **Placement:** align every field to the same left edge, baseline family, interval, and right-stub grid; allow the stamp to overlap the divider or lower grid slightly without obscuring values.
- **Material:** crisp black, charcoal, soft grey, or one quiet palette-relative ink printed into matte paper; no glow, UI styling, or detached overlay.
- **Script:** localise field labels as well as values into a native ticket/editorial equivalent for the resolved locale. Preserve natural Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Latin construction rather than silently defaulting labels to English.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.

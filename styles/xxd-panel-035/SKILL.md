# XXD Panel 035 · 明艳像素积木收藏品重构

Turn each supplied photograph into one vivid premium block-art collectible. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes. First understand the source's identity, action, relation, and implication, then rebuild the defining subject as one recognisable LEGO/Minecraft-like voxel sculpture through block proportion, modular geometry, assembly logic, clear silhouette, layered thickness, vivid source-derived colour, matte ABS plastic, visible joints, and believable contact shadows. The environment retreats into a quiet low-information field with only a necessary base, plane, or shadow.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; source-adaptive ordinary canvases; exact 50/50 paired geometry; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections may refine aesthetics, copy hierarchy, and acceptance criteria but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve identity, action, function, emotion, and relation through at least three source-specific cues while rebuilding the subject as one modular voxel/block sculpture. It is not a full Minecraft environment, loose-brick floor, forced mascot, generic game asset, low-poly mesh, children's interface, toy-shop render, smooth monolith, or e-commerce product shot.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 035 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relation → preserve at least three defining cues across contour, pose, direction, proportion, opening, overlap, functional part, colour, and relational distance → convert the subject into a hierarchy of cubic and modular units → establish one clean silhouette with source-earned local exaggeration → assign vivid, high-purity source-derived block colours → expose believable seams, thickness, levels, and contact shadows in matte ABS plastic → keep one subject as the absolute focal point on a much lighter quiet field → align one concise native title and only useful pixel or instruction-manual microcopy to the silhouette, base, or whitespace**.

The collectible quality comes from treating voxel and building-block logic as physical design construction rather than a video-game filter. LEGO, Minecraft-like voxel grammar, matte injection-moulded ABS, collectible toy sculpture, instruction diagrams, and modern editorial display are disciplines rather than licences to copy a branded set or game scene.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the block silhouette, modular hierarchy, local exaggeration, colour identity, seam placement, base relation, or annotation. Also reject complex Minecraft terrain, castles or forests added without evidence, scattered bricks, game HUD, pixel sprites, low-poly faceting, glossy CGI, metal or glass, neon, pastel toy colour, forced faces, and commercial packaging.

## 035 visual system

### One recognisable modular subject

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across contour, pose/orientation, function, proportion, material, colour, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, or relationship distance.
- Delete irrelevant detail and retain the structure, direction, silhouette, functional parts, opening, overlap, distance, and relation that make the source memorable.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth gesture; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and negative shape; landscapes keep one source-specific terrain or spatial relation.
- Rebuild with voxels, bricks, modular geometry, and plausible assembly logic rather than drawing square pixels over a cartoon. Recognition must survive thumbnail scale.
- Do not anthropomorphise animals, plants, buildings, vehicles, objects, or landscapes unless the source itself supports that reading.

### One focal collectible and a quiet field

- Keep one block-built subject as the absolute visual core with a clean silhouette and clear modular hierarchy.
- Use only a minimal base, plane, or soft contact shadow needed to support it. Do not reconstruct the full environment or add unrelated props, terrain, or scattered pieces.
- Let source direction and balance decide centred or off-centre placement, while a much lighter, simpler background supplies generous whitespace.
- Align annotation to the subject silhouette, base, block seam, or whitespace axis. The connection should feel designed, not pasted on.

### Matte ABS and credible assembly

- Use real matte ABS-like plastic with smooth flat faces, restrained soft reflections, visible module seams, credible thickness, stepped layers, and natural contact shadows.
- Every module must appear connectable or supportable. Keep joints, offsets, overlaps, and weight distribution plausible without turning the image into a literal construction manual.
- Use soft clear light that keeps colour vivid and reveals thickness and structure without dramatic studio gloss.
- Reject metallic or glass material, mirror highlights, excessive subsurface glow, over-polished CGI, mushy seamless blobs, cheap game rendering, and low-poly triangular facets.

### Vivid source-derived block colours

- Extract the source's most recognisable and spirited colours, then clarify them into high-purity, high-saturation block colours with clean hue boundaries.
- Preserve the source's combined colour identity while allowing the subject to become brighter and more energetic. Use area, value, and module grouping to maintain hierarchy.
- Keep the background clearly lighter and simpler so the saturated block subject remains the absolute focus.
- Avoid fixed primary-colour kits, neon, fluorescent hues, candy gradients, Morandi palettes, pastelisation, grey haze, muddy colour, and saturation reduced merely to appear tasteful.

## Copy behaves like modular auxiliary design

035 text is a restrained auxiliary layer derived from subject identity, action, emotion, relation, or supported meaning—not a toy logo, game UI, retail label, detached caption block, or decorative pixel font.

- Automatic copy derives one concise title from subject identity, action, emotion, relation, or supported meaning without inventing a brand, set number, game level, or product line.
- Prefer one restrained title plus zero to two grounded tag words or micro-notes. Numbers appear only when supplied or reliably established.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 035 construction:

- **Role:** a small information layer that supports the collectible without competing with it.
- **Voice:** concise, clear, lively, precise, and contemporary.
- **Construction:** one short title plus zero to two useful tags or micro-notes; the block subject remains the visual centre.
- **Placement:** align with the silhouette, base, block seam, module rhythm, or active whitespace.
- **Material:** restrained modular, pixel, or instruction-manual typography; never a game HUD, toy logo, oversized arcade font, faux set number, or retail packaging.
- **Script:** use native construction, joining, direction, punctuation, spacing, and legibility for the resolved Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, or Latin locale.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.
